# Vision Brief: [Name]

## The Problem
What pain, frustration, or missed opportunity exists today? Who is affected and what happens if nothing changes?

## Who Feels It
Who specifically experiences this problem? What's their role, situation, or context?

## The Vision
What does the world look like when this is solved? Describe the ideal outcome in plain language — not the solution, but the result.

## Key Capabilities
What must the solution be able to do? (3-5 bullets, written from the user's perspective)

## What Success Looks Like
How will you know it's working? Think business outcomes, not technical metrics.

## Constraints & Context
What's already in place? What are the boundaries (budget, timeline, existing tools, audience)?

## Open Questions
What's still unclear or needs more thought?
